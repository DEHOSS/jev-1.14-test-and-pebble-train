Jev 1.14 sweep-into tests

Three calls on saved sweep-into clips. Each clip sent 8 frames. A match means the result was the side with the higher ground-truth return. Tie counted as a miss whenever the returns differed.

Match rate

The first 10 pairs are the earliest checkpoints. The other two runs use the same 35 pairs, taken about every 40 feedback labels.

What the model answered

Most misses in the unthresholded stride-40 run are ties. Keeping a tie only when its probability is above 60% turns some of those into A or B and raises the match count from 13 to 20. Wrong-side answers rise from 2 to 5.

Match rate by return gap

Larger gaps are easier. On the thresholded run, 8 of the 10 pairs with a return gap of at least 3 were matched.
